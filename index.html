FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I really get from zero to B1 in six months?

With 30 minutes every day, you'll put in around 90 hours of focused, structured practice. Results depend on your background and consistency. Polku's weekly mock tests show your real progress, so you'll know when you're ready to book the exam.

How does the free trial work?

Your first 3 days are completely free with full access to every feature. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, your subscription continues at €49 per month through the App Store or Google Play. You can cancel anytime from your store account settings.

Should I choose Finnish or Swedish?

Both are official languages of Finland, and the YKI test is available in each. Finnish is spoken by most people in Finland; Swedish can be a good fit if you live on the coast or already know another Scandinavian language. You can switch tracks at any time.

What if I miss a day?

Life happens. Your plan rebalances automatically, and streak freezes keep your progress safe. Flashcard reviews wait for you; nothing is lost.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download the next week of lessons and study on the train or anywhere without a connection. The AI speaking practice needs internet.

Is Polku affiliated with the official YKI test?

No. Polku is an independent preparation app. The YKI test is organised by the Finnish National Agency for Education (Opetushallitus) and the University of Jyväskylä.
